WHO I AM

I am Federico Monaco: sociologist (PhD in Social and Political Systems, University of Parma; degree in Sociology, La Sapienza) with twenty years across universities, hospitals, public administration, EU institutions and space-analog missions. My thread: turning knowledge into practice that teams can repeat — through simulation, open data, immersive and distance learning, and facilitation.

Fluent English (C2), plus Dutch, French and German. Tools of the trade: Moodle, Blender, Gravity Sketch, WordPress, Drupal, GitHub, Google Workspace.
